    Question Possible Solutions Resource
    Why is my printer not printing?
    • Check the connections of your computer and printer.
    • Ensure the printer is plugged in and turned on.
    • The paper must be properly loaded into the paper tray.

    How do I find my printer’s error messages?
    • Look for the printer’s display panel and see if any messages are on there.
    • They should say an error code and what the error is.
    • They may be associated with the printer or fax machine, if applicable.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    Why is my printer not turning on?
    • Ensure the printer is plugged in and connected into a wall socket.
    • Try using another wire if it’s still not turning on.
    • Also try connecting into another socket.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    Why is my printer printing blank pages?
    • There are multiple possible reasons. Ink may be low or empty.
    • Also check your print preview to make sure it shows what is supposed to be printed.
    • The print head may need to be cleaned as well.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    Why is my printer so slow?
    • Other users may be trying to print as well, causing the queue to load up significantly.
    • Printer drivers may need to be updated.
    • If you’re trying to print a large document, such as a picture, consider reducing the print quality.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    Why is my printer printing blurry pages?
    • Check the ink levels of your printer. Ensure it is not low.
    • The paper loaded into the printer should be designed for printing, dry, and free of wrinkles.
    • The printer head may need to be cleaned.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    How I do change my printing color?
    • There are multiple ways to do this. In an application like Microsoft Word, you can go to File -> Print -> Printer Properties of your printer.
    • You should see options of “Black & White” & “Color”.
    • Through an operating system like Windows 11, it can be done from “Settings” -> “Printers & Scanners”. Click your printer name and then select Printer Preferences -> Paper/Quality to change the printing color.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    How do I know if my printer’s ink is low?
    • When printing, there should be notifications on your computer screen and printer display saying the ink is low.
    • You should also see icons and percentages to indicate how low they are.
    • It may be noticed by printing blurry documents.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    Why is my printer not being seen by my computer?
    • Check the connections between the computer and printer. If wired, ensure the connections are tight.
    • If wireless, they should both be connected to the same network as each other.
    • Consider trying a different wire or port.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    How do I know if I’m putting paper correctly into my printer?
    • Insert and line up the paper with the guides into the paper feed inside the tray.
    • Ensure the paper is dry, free of wrinkles, and fits into the tray.
    • Also make sure the tray is not overloaded to prevent paper jams.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    How do I print a test page from my printer?
    • On your computer, go into Settings -> Printers & Scanners.
    • Look for and click on your printer to look for options.
    • Choose “Print Test Page”.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    How do I see my printer’s queue?
    • On your computer, go into Settings -> Printers & Scanners.
    • From there, click on your printer to see an option to open the printer queue.
    • You can also find it on your printer’s display panel and find an option to see the queue from its main menu.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    How do I replace my printer’s ink cartridges?
    • Open the ink cartridge cover of the printer by pulling it back with one hand.
    • On the top of the cartridges to replace, press down on its lever to pull it out.
    • Then push in the new cartridges to replace. Ensure they fit and are compatible with the printer.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    The printer is not scanning my document. What should I do?
    • Make sure the paper is properly placed into the scanner unit.
    • If you’re attempting to scan through a network, check to see if there’s a stable connection between the printer and computer.
    • Ink levels may also need to be checked.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    I updated my computer, but I am no longer able to print. What can be done?
    • Check the printer drivers. They may be outdated and require you to update them.
    • Verify there’s a connection between the printer and computer.
    • You can also try restarting the printer spooler on Windows by typing “Services” into the search bar, highlight “Printer Spooler”, then right-click on it to choose the “Restart” option.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    I made a copy for my document, but the copy is blank. Why is that?
    • The document may be facing the wrong way, causing it to copy the blank side.
    • Ink levels may possibly be low.
    • The print head may also need to be cleaned.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    Why is my printer having a paper jam?
    • The paper loaded into the printer may be loaded incorrectly.
    • There may be too much paper loaded into the paper tray.
    • The paper size or type may also be incorrect and not designed for the printing you want to do.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    I have multiple print jobs, but they are stuck in the queue. What should I try?
    • Check your printer’s panel screen and look for an option to check the printer queue.
    • They may be waiting to be printed while another job is ongoing.
    • The printer may have low ink and paper while printing other jobs.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    How do I find drivers for my printer?
    • On Windows, you can right-click the Start icon and choose Device Manager.
    • From there, look for Printers and select that to find the name of your printer.
    • On a MAC, find the Apple button. Choose System Preferences -> Printers & Scanners. Choose your printer, then Options and Supplies.
    		 Click Here For More Information
    I am using an application, but it is not letting me print. What can be done?
    • The app may need to be updated to the latest version.
    • The application may need to be restarted, but ensure you save your wanted work first.
    • It could also simply be that the computer and printer are not connected to each other.
